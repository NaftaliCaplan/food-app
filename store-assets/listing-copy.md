## Full description (Google Play + App Store, both allow up to ~4000 chars)
CBA (Color-Blind Assist) helps you make simple everyday color decisions your eyes might not be able to make confidently on their own.

Four tools, one camera:
• Is it ready? — Point your camera at food to check if it's ripe, cooked, or safe to eat.
• Does it match? — Build a digital wardrobe and get outfit suggestions that actually go together.
• How's my outfit? — Check what you're wearing right now for a quick match/clash read.
• What color is it? — Point at any light or indicator and get a plain-language answer: red, green, amber, and more.

Built from the ground up for colorblind users, not retrofitted with a color filter. Every result is a direct, worded answer — no relying on a color you can't distinguish.

Privacy, kept simple: no accounts, no logins, no ads, no analytics or tracking of any kind. Photos are sent once to an AI service to get your answer and are never stored on our servers. Wardrobe and profile photos stay only on your own device.

Free to use.
