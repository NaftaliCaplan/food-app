Privacy Policy for CBA

Last updated: November 14, 2026

CBA ("the app," "we," "our") is a mobile app that helps identify colors and match clothing for colorblind users. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Camera Photos

The app uses your camera for several features: checking food freshness, tagging wardrobe items, checking whether an outfit matches, and identifying the color of a status/indicator light.

  • For the food, outfit-match, and light-color checkers, your photo is sent once to Cloudflare, Inc. ("Cloudflare"), a cloud AI processing company, to generate a result. Our backend does not store these photos — once a result is returned, nothing is retained on our side.
  • For wardrobe items, your photo is also sent once to Cloudflare to automatically tag it (color, type, etc.), and is then saved only on your own device — never uploaded to any server or database we control. Deleting an item from your wardrobe deletes its photo immediately.
  • If you set up a style profile, a reference photo is saved locally on your device as part of that profile, the same way.

What We Don't Do

This app has no user accounts, no login, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no advertising, and does not sell or share your data with anyone. There's nothing to track you with, because nothing identifies you to us in the first place.

IP Address

Our backend briefly logs IP addresses (for a few minutes at a time) purely to prevent abuse of the AI service. This is never linked to any account or identity, because none exists.

Third-Party Processing

Photos sent for analysis are processed by Cloudflare Workers AI. Cloudflare's own privacy practices govern how they handle that data during processing; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
